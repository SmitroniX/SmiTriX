What it does

Train by tapping the body

Tap muscles, front and back, and get a targeted session. Warm-up, working, failure and drop sets, RPE or RIR, Olympic plates per side, and per-side reps.

Automatic progression

Suggests more reps or more weight next session, flags plateaus, schedules deload sessions and keeps your 1RM curves clean.

A rest timer you can hear

Vertical draining tick bar, custom chime presets, voice coach announcements, screen flash & sage edge glow when rest ends.

Routines and circuits

Fixed weekly plan or rolling rotation, 3D routine folders, supersets, timed circuits with rounds, starter plans and instant exercise swaps.

Progress that is real

Estimated 1RM, personal records, volume charts, streaks that survive rest days, muscle recovery map, 20 3D medals and shareable monthly recaps.

Body and photos

Body weight curve with goal tracking, ten body measurements plus custom ones, progress photo timeline with before/after compare and muscle map mode.

Nutrition & recovery (beta)

560+ built-in foods, calorie ring & macro bars, water vessel, protein shakes, supplements, fasting timer, plus sauna, cold plunge and guided breathwork.

1,300+ animated exercises

Full animation & step-by-step instructions, equipment filters, training places (Gym, Home, Outdoors), plus custom exercises with your own media.

Journal and notes

Session notes, pinned per-exercise cues, calendar training journal with folders, and form check video vault.

12 calculators & AI Coach

Built-in 1RM, plates, BMI, macros, body fat, warm-up pyramid, RPE load, strength standards, Wilks, FFMI, protein & water calculators — plus optional BYOK AI Coach.

Your data, yours

Import from Hevy, Strong, FitNotes or CSV. Export full JSON backups anytime. No login on website or demo; optional cloud sync inside the Android app or self-host via Docker.

15 languages, your way

Dark, OLED and Light themes, warm dots or subtle grid backgrounds, custom accent colors, kg or lb, built for sweaty gym hands.

Screens

Real screens across training, progress, nutrition, body timeline and settings. Tap any phone to inspect full size.

Your data stays under your control

On the website and live demo, SmiTriX asks for zero login and saves everything strictly in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Inside the Android app you can train with optional cloud sync or pair to your own self-hosted Docker instance — and export your complete JSON vault anytime.
